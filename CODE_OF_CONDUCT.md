# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Project participants are expected to collaborate respectfully, focus on the
technical work, and keep discussions constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Use clear, professional language.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about problems.
- Accept technical disagreement as part of design review.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, security, and release timing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private security reports before coordinated disclosure.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
